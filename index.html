Airin Akter

PhD Candidate in Geography, University of Florida

I am a PhD student in the Department of Geography at the University of Florida, advised by Dr. Corene Matyas. My research focuses on the spatial patterns of rainfall in tropical cyclones. I use satellite precipitation data and atmospheric reanalysis data to study what shapes these patterns.

Before my PhD, I worked in GIS and humanitarian mapping in Bangladesh, including community-based mapping for cyclone evacuation planning.
